Event ticketing platform

Full stack · Web Development 01418441

A ticketing site with two roles. Attendees browse and search events, buy tickets with an in-app coin balance, and get a QR code for each ticket; organizers create and edit events, upload images, and scan tickets at the door. Every ticket carries a UUID, and the check-in endpoint verifies the ticket exists, belongs to that organizer's event, and hasn't already been used — so a screenshot of someone else's QR code won't get you in.
